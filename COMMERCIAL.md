## When you need a commercial license

You likely need a commercial license if you want to:

- Deploy CVM or a derivative as a SaaS, advisory tool, or research product **without** publishing your modifications under AGPL.
- Integrate the scoring engine into a closed-source investment platform, fintech application, or internal analytics stack.
- Use the CVM framework in published, branded, or paid investment research without academic citation, or in any way that suggests endorsement.
- Remove or modify the attribution and disclaimer requirements.
- Receive indemnification, a defined support SLA, or contractual warranties that AGPL explicitly disclaims.

You do **not** need a commercial license to:

- Use the public hosted tool at the URL listed in the README.
- Self-host an unmodified copy for personal investment research.
- Fork the repository, modify it, and publish your fork under AGPL-3.0.
- Cite the methodology in academic papers, blog posts, or editorial commentary with proper attribution.

## A note on the methodology

The Concentric Value Model — its layer structure, dimension definitions, and weighting derivation — is original research. Reproducing the framework in derivative tools, training materials, or commercial products without attribution is not authorized by the AGPL alone. Commercial licenses include explicit rights to reference the methodology in product documentation.

## Terms
